Privacy Policy for CA-GABAY

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the CA-GABAY mobile application.

App Name: CA-GABAY
Developer: Bakawan Data Analytics Inc.
Application ID: com.fdsap.cagabay

CA-GABAY is developed and published by Bakawan Data Analytics Inc.

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use the CA-GABAY mobile application, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and other applicable privacy laws and regulations.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, mailing address, institution, staff ID, and contact details.
  • Usage information such as your interactions with the application and preferences.
  • Device information such as your IP address, device type, operating system, and other information necessary for application security and compatibility.
  • Precise or approximate location information when required for specific application functions.

Location Information

CA-GABAY may access and collect the current location of your device for specific operational and security purposes.

Location information may be collected in the following situations:

  • During login – CA-GABAY may capture the device's current location when a user logs in. This information may be used for security, audit records, and authorized operational purposes.
  • During loan application creation or processing – CA-GABAY may capture the current location associated with the creation or processing of a loan application when location information is required for authorized business operations.

Location information may include geographic coordinates such as latitude and longitude, as well as related information necessary to determine the location and accuracy of the device.

CA-GABAY does not continuously track or monitor the location of your device through these functions. Location information is obtained only when required by the applicable application function or authorized transaction.

Access to collected location information is limited to authorized personnel and systems and is subject to appropriate security and access controls.

Location Permission

CA-GABAY may request permission from your device to access location information when a feature requiring location information is used. Location permissions are managed through your device's operating system.

The availability and accuracy of location information may depend on your device settings, location services, network connectivity, and permissions granted to the application.

Granting location permission does not mean that CA-GABAY continuously monitors or tracks your device. Location information is collected only in connection with the specific application functions described in this Privacy Policy.

Camera Access

The CA-GABAY application uses the camera feature to:

  • Capture photos for profile pictures.
  • Verify user authenticity to prevent misuse and maintain the security of the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing and improving our application and services.
  • Authenticating users and helping protect accounts against unauthorized access or misuse.
  • Maintaining security, transaction, and audit records.
  • Supporting authorized loan application and operational processes.
  • Providing application functions that require location information.
  • Personalizing your experience and displaying relevant content.
  • Engaging with you and responding to your inquiries.
  • Analyzing usage trends and optimizing application performance, reliability, and security.

Personal information will only be processed for declared, specified, and legitimate purposes and will be limited to information that is necessary and appropriate for those purposes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Personal information, including location information when collected, will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, meet legitimate business requirements, or comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted, disposed of, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable data retention and disposal policies.

4. Access and Disclosure

Access to personal information collected through CA-GABAY is restricted to authorized personnel, systems, and service providers that require such information for legitimate and authorized purposes.

Personal information will not be disclosed to unauthorized parties. Where information must be disclosed or shared, such processing will be performed in accordance with applicable privacy laws, organizational policies, and appropriate security safeguards.

5. Security

We are committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal information processed through CA-GABAY.

We use reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures to help protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse, or other unlawful processing.
